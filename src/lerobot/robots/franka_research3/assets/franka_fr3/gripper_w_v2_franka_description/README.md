# corenetic gripper v2 (vendor package `gripper_w_v2_franka_description`)

Gripper part of `fr3_corenetic_gripper.urdf` / `.xml` since 2026-10-12. It replaces the old
`URDF_franka_corenetic_gripper` package, whose fingers were wrong: its TCP sat 191 mm below the flange.
This one puts `link_gripper_tcp` (= `corenetic_gripper_ee`) 171.99 mm below the flange, at the contact point
of the closed fingers. That agrees with the vendor's Franka Desk config (0.17199) and a tape measure (~170 mm).
The finger meshes do not match the real gripper along the tool axis. With the sensor fingertips and the
gripper closed, the meshes put the nose 184 mm below the flange (EE z +12). On 2026-10-13 a tape measured
about 175 mm. On the real gripper the EE therefore sits about 3 mm inside the nose, not at the pad centre.
Do not use the meshes to locate points on the fingertip, such as the pivot insert's socket. The laser measures
that socket at EE (1.45, −0.78, −5.06) mm in the TCP frame while the insert is gripped, with a 35 mm gap
between the pads: the FR3 pivot about a fixed ball, 2026-10-14, and a caliper reads flange face → socket
166.9 mm. The earlier −10.3 mm came from the laser end to end and included the world → fr3_base edge.
The repo URDF matches Thor's replay model (`box_api/replay_p0_arm_only_20260908/model.urdf`) frame for frame.

- `urdf/gripper_w_v2_franka_description.urdf`: the vendor file, verbatim, for reference only. It does not
  load as it stands: two joints (`joint_franka_corenetic_gripper_4`, `joint_lt_gripper_base`) name child
  links it never defines, and its mesh paths point at `meshes/visual` / `meshes/collision`.
- `meshes/`: one copy of the vendor meshes (its visual and collision meshes are byte-identical). The old
  camera mount `link_franka_corenetic_gripper_3` is left out: it was removed from the robot, and the
  SMR/camera adaptor (`../corenetic_smr_cam/`) replaces it.
- What the repo files change from the vendor URDF (regenerated by `~/cad/corenetic_smr_cam/rebuild_gripper_v2.py`):
  - The two dangling joints and the old mount are dropped.
  - `corenetic_gripper_ee` (same pose as `link_gripper_tcp`) hangs below a compatibility frame
    `link_lt_gripper_tcp`, the old package's TCP axes: R_x(π) from `link_gripper_tcp`, R_x(π) back to the EE.
    The marker->TCP bundles land on that frame, and the opencv_kalibr tracker and replay hop from it to the EE.
    Without it, Generate EE Trajectory and real replay both refuse to run. Renaming the hop to `link_gripper_tcp`
    instead would flip every EE orientation without an error.
  - Links the vendor left without a collision get their visual mesh as collision.
  - MJCF only:
    - Jointed links get 1 g stand-in masses. MuJoCo needs them; the vendor lumps all gripper mass into
      `link_gripper_base` and gives the moving links 1e-16 kg.
    - Contacts inside one side's linkage are excluded. The hulls overlap by ~7 mm, which threw the drive
      off its target.
    - `ctrl` 0..1 maps to the drive angle 0..1.44339 rad (the knuckle limit); 0 = closed.

Inertial caveats (vendor numbers, not measured):

- `link_sensor_ft`'s mass and inertia are the old package's `link_franka_corenetic_gripper_2` numbers,
  copied verbatim.
- `base_link` is now 75.8 g (old: 28.1 g).

The real end-effector load is identified on the robot instead (`tools/fr3/fr3_payload_identification_runtime.py`).
Franka Desk configs from it (10-12; flange frame, TCP z 0.17199 / yaw −45° as the vendor's):

| Desk name | mass | CoM (mm) | closed loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corenetic v2.0 BOX + cam | 0.9755 kg | (12.8, −19.0, 56.9) | Fz −0.17 N |
| Corenetic v2.0 BOX + cam + SMR | 1.1108 kg | (16.8, −22.2, 60.9) | Fz −0.11 N |

The URDF/MJCF masses stay CAD (0.867 / 1.007 kg). The Desk mass is the load that best explains the
torques with libfranka's arm model and no sensor offsets. That is what the robot needs, but it is not
a weighing: the physical load is only known to ~±60 g.
